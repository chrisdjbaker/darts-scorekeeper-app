Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy covers the Darts Scorekeeper app for iPhone and iPad (“the app”).

We don’t collect your data

The developer does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The developer runs no servers.

What stays on your device

Player names, the game in progress, finished games and stats are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Spoken scores and TV

If you turn on spoken scores, the words are made on your device by iOS. Showing the scoreboard on a TV uses AirPlay or a cable between your own devices.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
